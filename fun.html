Concepts I made because I wanted to see them move.

None of this shipped. These are spatial interface studies and concept films I make outside client work — usually to answer a question that's been bothering me, occasionally just because an idea looked good in my head and I wanted to check.

Motion is the point. A spatial interface that reads fine as a still frame can fall apart the moment it has to respond to a person, so I tend to skip straight to animating it. Hover any of these to play.
